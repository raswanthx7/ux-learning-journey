# Design Sprint Brief

A **Sprint Brief** is a preparation document shared with everyone attending a Design Sprint. It explains the challenge, goals, people, resources, project context, and schedule so participants can prepare.

---

## 1. Sprint Challenge 

The Sprint Challenge introduces the **main objective of the sprint**.

It tells the team what challenge they are trying to solve.

**Example:**

> Build exciting new features for the cycling app in time for relaunch later in the year.

---

## 2. Key Deliverables 

Deliverables define **what the team wants to create by the end of the sprint**.

**Example:**

> A complete prototype that includes the feature update.

The deliverable is usually a **prototype**, not the final developed product.

---

## 3. Logistics 

Logistics provide the basic information about the sprint:

- Where will the sprint happen?
- When will it happen?
- Who is attending?
- Who is the Sprint Leader?

**Think:** Who + Where + When + Who is leading?

---

## 4. Approvers 

Approvers are the people who need to **review and approve the product or direction**.

**Example:**

> The CEO may need to approve the new cycling feature before launch.

---

## 5. Resources 

Resources are the **people and other resources needed** for the project.

**Example:**

> Engineering team members may be needed to develop the new feature after the sprint.

For longer projects, additional resources may need to be arranged.

---

## 6. Project Overview 

The project overview explains the **current situation of the project**.

Include:

- Current project status
- Existing roadblocks
- Early wins
- Estimated launch plan

**Think:**

> Where are we now, and what is happening with the project?

---

## 7. Sprint Schedule 

The Sprint Schedule shows the **hour-by-hour plan** for the sprint.

It includes:

- Day 1 activities
- Day 2 activities
- Day 3 activities
- Day 4 activities
- Day 5 activities
- Break times

**Goal:** Let participants know what to expect and give them enough time to prepare.

---

# Easy Way to Remember

**Challenge → Deliverables → Logistics → Approvers → Resources → Project Overview → Schedule**

---

## One-Line Definition

> **A Sprint Brief is a document that tells the sprint team what the challenge is, what they need to create, who is involved, what resources are needed, and how the sprint will run.**
