# Design Sprint Planning

Before starting a Design Sprint, the team needs to prepare properly.

## 7 Steps to Prepare a Design Sprint

### 1. User Research 🔍

- Conduct user research first.
- Focus on the user problems related to the sprint.
- Get a rough understanding of what the sprint will focus on.

**Goal:** Understand the users and the problem before the sprint starts.

---

### 2. Talk to Experts 🗣️

- Invite colleagues or industry experts.
- Schedule short talks with them.
- Their insights help clarify the sprint challenge.

**Goal:** Get useful knowledge before ideation.

---

### 3. Find the Right Space 🏢

The space should have:

- Whiteboards
- Space for sticky notes
- Good acoustics
- Accessibility accommodations
- Comfortable, movable furniture

**Goal:** Create an environment where everyone can participate effectively.

---

### 4. Gather Supplies ✏️

Prepare:

- Markers
- Sticky notes
- Paper
- Snacks
- Water
- Other materials needed for the sprint

**Goal:** Make sure everyone has what they need.

---

### 5. Establish Sprint Rules 📋

Set ground rules before the sprint.

For example:

- No phones during activities
- Stay focused
- Everyone participates
- Respect others' ideas

**Goal:** Keep the team focused and working effectively.

---

### 6. Plan Introductions 🤝

Design Sprints usually involve cross-functional teams.

Use an icebreaker to:

- Help people get comfortable
- Encourage collaboration
- Put the team into a user-focused mindset

**Example:**  
Ask everyone to share a time when they called customer service to complain.

---

### 7. Plan After the Sprint 📸

Think about what happens after the sprint.

Document the sprint by:

- Taking photos
- Saving sticky notes
- Recording important ideas
- Documenting decisions
- Planning next steps

**Goal:** Make sure the sprint's work and insights are useful after the sprint ends.

---

## Easy Way to Remember

**Research → Experts → Space → Supplies → Rules → Introductions → Post-Sprint Plan**

### Key Takeaway

Good Design Sprint preparation means making sure the **right research, people, environment, materials, rules, and follow-up plan** are ready before the sprint begins.
